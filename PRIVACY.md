# Privacy Policy

**Solar MCP**

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Solar MCP is an open-source Model Context Protocol (MCP) server developed and maintained by Gauthier Rammault.

The service provides UV index forecasts and atmospheric ozone concentration data based on geographic coordinates.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when users interact with Solar MCP.

- **Source code:** https://github.com/g0thier/solar-mcp
- **Service:** https://solar-mcp.onrender.com
- **Contact:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/gauthier-rammault/

## 2. Information Processed

Solar MCP processes the following information to respond to requests:

- Geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) supplied by the user or MCP client.
- Technical information necessary to process requests and deliver responses.
- Technical logs that may be generated by the hosting infrastructure.

Solar MCP does not require users to create an account or provide their name or email address to request UV and ozone forecasts.

Geographic coordinates may represent a user's current location or another location of interest. They should not be assumed to identify the user's actual location.

## 3. Purpose of Processing

Information is processed to:

- Retrieve UV index forecasts.
- Retrieve atmospheric ozone concentration data.
- Generate location-based environmental information.
- Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the service.

Geographic coordinates are necessary to provide location-specific results.

## 4. Hosting and Third-Party Services

Solar MCP is hosted using Render.

Requests to the service may pass through Render's infrastructure and be subject to its technical logging and security processes.

Solar MCP also retrieves environmental information from external data sources.

Users should consult the privacy policies of relevant third-party providers for additional information about their processing practices.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Solar MCP is designed to process geographic requests and return environmental data.

The retention of technical request information, including any infrastructure logs, depends on the server configuration and hosting provider.

The developer should verify and document the applicable logging configuration and retention periods before public deployment.

## 6. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers and environmental data services to the extent necessary to operate Solar MCP.

Solar MCP is not intended to sell users' personal information or use geographic requests for advertising or profiling.

## 7. Security

Reasonable technical measures should be maintained to protect the service and its infrastructure.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## 8. International Data Processing

Because Solar MCP uses third-party hosting and environmental data providers, information may be processed outside the user's country of residence.

Applicable data protection rights and safeguards depend on the actual processing arrangements and relevant law.
